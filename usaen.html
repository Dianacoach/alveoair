Focused not only on helping prevent respiratory setbacks but also on guiding the client through structured weekly improvement plans with realistic, achievable short-term goals. These plans are designed to promote healthier respiratory habits, safer therapy management and measurable day-to-day progress through respiratory muscle training, breathing exercises, improved sleep support with proper equipment and nutrition, correct medication administration and gradual oxygen weaning goals as the client improve.

On the psychological side, these services focus not only on improving comfort and safety at home but also on helping the client feel more confident and independent during daily activities outside the home. This includes understanding oxygen duration, portable oxygen systems, respiratory equipment use and better day-to-day respiratory planning.

Therapy, support and guidance are provided through WhatsApp via video, telephone and email in the management of oxygen therapy, CPAP/BiPAP and tracheostomy care. Guidance. Also available for decisions related to purchasing or renting respiratory equipment and new updates.

Available services include: Basic, Preventive and Flexible plans. These services are designed to complement physician care by providing the client, caregivers, family members and daily support personnel with professional respiratory guidance whenever needed, whether during recovery or as part of ongoing daily respiratory support.
