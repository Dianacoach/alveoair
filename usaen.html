Focused on helping prevent respiratory setbacks and ineffective therapy management through structured weekly improvement plans with realistic and achievable short-term goals. These plans are designed to promote better respiratory habits, safer therapy management and measurable day-to-day progress. Services focus not only on improving comfort and safety at home, but also on helping individuals feel more confident and independent during daily activities outside the home, including understanding oxygen duration, portable oxygen systems, respiratory equipment use and better day-to-day respiratory planning. Support and guidance are provided through video, telephone calls and email, offering remote respiratory coaching designed to improve understanding and confidence in the management of oxygen therapy, CPAP/BiPAP, tracheostomy care, inhaled medications and respiratory equipment. Services are aimed at helping reduce confusion, errors, delays, stress and potential respiratory-related complications while promoting safer and more effective respiratory habits. Guidance is also available for decisions related to purchasing or renting respiratory equipment. Available services include the Basic, Preventive, and Flexible plans. These services are designed to complement your physician’s care by providing patients, caregivers, family members, or daily support personnel with access to professional and specialized respiratory guidance whenever it is needed most or as part of daily respiratory support.
