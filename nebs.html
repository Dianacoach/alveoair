Inhaler (MDI) and Nebulizer Therapy: Spacer, Mask, and Tracheostomy Use Explained

Clear guidance for children, adults, and tracheostomy care

Children & Limited Inhalation

  • Used with a face mask when patients cannot form a tight seal on a mouthpiece.
  • Ideal for infants, young children, and adults with poor coordination or weakness.
  • Eliminates the need to synchronize inhalation with actuation.
  • Improves medication delivery to the lungs and reduces loss in the mouth/throat.
  • Mask should fit snugly over nose and mouth to avoid medication leakage.

Adult Use

  • Recommended for most adults to improve inhaler technique and drug delivery.
  • Removes coordination issues between pressing the inhaler and inhaling.
  • Allows slow, deep breathing for better lung deposition.
  • Reduces medication deposition in the oropharynx.
  • Particularly useful during shortness of breath episodes.

Tracheostomy Use

  • Can be adapted using a trach mask or spacer adapter.
  • Delivers medication directly into the airway bypassing upper airway structures.
  • Useful for patients unable to use standard inhalation techniques.
  • Ensures controlled and efficient delivery.
  • Monitoring is important for proper fit and effectiveness.

Children & Limited Inhalation

  • Delivered via mask when patients cannot use a mouthpiece effectively.
  • Requires minimal coordination—normal breathing is enough.
  • Ideal for young children, elderly, or cognitively impaired patients.
  • Provides continuous aerosol over several minutes.
  • Mask fit is important to reduce medication loss.

Adult Use

  • Can be used with mouthpiece (preferred) or mask.
  • Effective during moderate to severe respiratory distress.
  • Allows delivery of higher medication doses over time.
  • No coordination required—simple breathing is sufficient.
  • Useful when MDI technique is difficult.

Tracheostomy Use

  • Administered via trach collar or direct connection.
  • Delivers medication directly into lower airways.
  • Used for bronchodilation and secretion management.
  • Provides humidification when needed.
  • Proper setup is essential for effective delivery.
