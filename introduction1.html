Experience My Respiratory Coaching Program

A breathing technique that works well for one person may not be enough for another because the real cause of their difficulty may lie elsewhere. My respiratory coaching focuses on understanding the whole person, not just the condition. Together, we identify the obstacles affecting your quality of life, make practical adjustments tailored to your individual circumstances, and follow your progress closely with structured guidance and ongoing support. The goal is not simply to teach information that can be found online, but to help you apply the right solutions to your specific situation and achieve meaningful, measurable improvements week after week.

Through structured weekly improvement plans and realistic short-term goals, respiratory coaching is designed to help improve respiratory habits, therapy management, confidence, and day to day quality of life. Support is available through video calls, telephone and email, providing personalized guidance for oxygen therapy, CPAP/BiPAP, tracheostomy care, inhaled medications, respiratory equipment and daily respiratory planning.

Services are designed to help reduce confusion, prevent common therapy mistakes, improve confidence when using respiratory equipment and promote safer, more effective respiratory routines at home and during daily activities outside the home. Guidance is also available when considering the purchase or rental of respiratory equipment.

Whether you are a patient, caregiver, family member or support professional, these services complement your physician's care by providing practical respiratory guidance, ongoing support, and individualized coaching tailored to your specific needs.
